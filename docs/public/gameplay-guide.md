# Wargame X — Game Rules

Wargame X is a text-based, browser multiplayer RTS set on a massive shared world where every player builds an empire, competes for position, and fights for long-term advantage. There is no single final victory: the map keeps growing, the world keeps moving, and your goal is to climb the leaderboard through smart expansion, strong logistics, and well-timed aggression.

## The World

The world of Wargame X is large, procedurally generated, and shaped from a single seed. It is effectively infinite, with new territory generated as players explore farther from known land.

Seven biome types create different strategic opportunities across the map. Jungles are rich in carbon, mountains are heavy with iron, deserts and oceans are prime sources of oil, and crystal is especially valuable in tundra and desert regions. You begin with only a small revealed area, so growth depends on pushing outward and securing better ground.

## Your Empire

You build and manage your empire through three core parts:

**Bases** are structures built on tiles. Your first base is your origin, and every base acts as a supply source, a spawn point, and a hub for processing resources.

**Forces** are the armies you command across the map. They can move, attack, defend, or hold position, and can be made up of ground, air, and naval units.

**Resources** are the foundation of expansion. The four key resources are Carbon, Iron, Crystal, and Oil, each used for different parts of growth, construction, and warfare. Resource deposits regenerate over time, up to each tile's natural capacity.

## Commanding Forces

Forces move by order and advance tile by tile with each game tick. Terrain matters: plains are fast to cross, while mountains and swamps slow movement significantly.

Air units ignore terrain penalties, letting them travel efficiently across difficult ground. Naval units are restricted to water and can only operate where seas and oceans allow.

If enemy forces end up on the same tile, combat commences. Friendly forces can stack together on the same tile, allowing you to mass strength where it matters most.

## Expansion and Resources

Exploration is essential. As your forces move, they reveal the fog-of-war and expose new land, new threats, and new opportunities.

To gather resources, build extractors on or near strong resource tiles. Carbon, Iron, Crystal, and Oil regenerate naturally, and extractors draw from what is currently stored on a tile each tick. The best rewards usually lie beyond your starting zone, so expansion into mountain ranges, jungles, deserts, and distant frontiers is a major part of competitive play.

## Research and Alliances

Research lets you improve your empire over time by unlocking stronger units, better structures, and new capabilities.

Alliances let players cooperate for mutual gain. Allies can share vision, coordinate offensives, and combine pressure against common enemies. Trade adds another layer by allowing players to exchange resources directly when it serves their plans.

## Attrition

Armies cannot operate at peak strength forever without support. Forces that push too far from a supply source gradually lose readiness over time.

Harsh terrain also wears armies down. Units encamped in mountains, swamp, or ice suffer additional strain, and reduced readiness lowers combat effectiveness. Keeping supply lines stable is just as important as winning battles.

## Scoring and the Leaderboard

Wargame X is open-ended and competitive. You earn points through expansion, military success, accumulated resource wealth, and control of territory.

The leaderboard tracks all active players, and the main objective is simple: keep improving your position. Because the world is persistent, there is no final end state. Empires rise, collapse, recover, and return to the contest.

## Quick Reference

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Move a force | Issue a move order to a target tile |
| Attack | Move your force onto a tile occupied by enemies |
| Extract resources | Build an extractor on or adjacent to a resource-rich tile |
| Build a base | Place a base structure on a buildable tile within your territory |
| Form alliance | Send an alliance proposal to another player |
| Trade | Open a trade offer with another player or ally |
| Research | Queue research at a base with research capability |
